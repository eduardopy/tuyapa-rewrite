Privacy Policy

Effective: February 23, 2026

This page describes how TUYA PA ("we," "us") handles information collected through this website. It does not describe our handling of protected health information (PHI) under HIPAA — that is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices, which is provided to you at our offices.

Information we collect through this site

  • Standard web server logs — IP address, browser type, pages visited — retained for security and troubleshooting.
  • Language preference, stored in your browser only (a "tuya-lang" entry in localStorage). It is not transmitted to us.
  • Integration metadata required to connect authorized services, such as the public keys exposed at /.well-known/jwks.json.

What we don't do

  • We do not run third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or cross-site trackers on this website.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect personal health information through this website. Please use the Patient Portal or call us for anything sensitive.

Sharing

We share information only with authorized partners and vendors as needed to provide services, and as required by law.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information we hold. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is fully secure; please do not include sensitive medical information in website forms or emails.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please call (772) 589-0300.
