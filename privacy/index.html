Privacy Policy

Effective: February 23, 2026

This page describes how TUYA PA ("we," "us") handles information collected through this website. It does not describe our handling of protected health information (PHI) under HIPAA — that is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices, available at our offices.

Information we collect

  • Messages you choose to send us through the contact form (your name, email, and the contents of your message).
  • Integration metadata required to connect authorized services (for example, public keys exposed at /.well-known/jwks.json).
  • Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) retained for security and troubleshooting.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries you send us through the contact form.
  • To operate and secure integrations with authorized services such as the Patient Portal.
  • To support care operations for our patient population, as permitted by applicable law and contracts.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with authorized partners and vendors as needed to provide services, and as required by law.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information we hold. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is fully secure; please do not include sensitive medical information in website forms or emails. For secure communication with your provider, use the Patient Portal.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us or call (772) 589-0300.
