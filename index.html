Our Services

Comprehensive care, with a particular focus on patients aged 65 and older.

Comprehensive Geriatric Care

Outstanding care for patients aged 65 and older, focused on prevention and quality of life — not just managing conditions.

Preventive Care

Regular check-ups and screenings to prevent illness or catch it at its earliest, most treatable stages.

Chronic Care Management

For Medicare patients with chronic conditions like diabetes, arthritis, or depression — monthly check-ins and coordinated specialist care.

Prescription Management

Safe, effective medication use — monitored for interactions, refills, and whether each prescription is achieving its purpose.

Primary Care

Annual physicals, routine screenings, immunizations, treatment for acute illness, and coordination with specialists when needed.

Patient-Focused Approach

We care for you like family — proactive reminders, thoughtful follow-ups, and care plans built around your life, not ours.

Frequently Asked Questions

For anything not covered here, please call us.

Which insurance plans do you accept?
We accept Medicare, UnitedHealthcare, WellMed, Devoted Health, and Humana. If your plan isn't listed, call us — we may still be able to help.
How can I reach my doctor between visits?
Send non-emergency messages through the secure Patient Portal. Our staff responds within one business day. For urgent issues, call the office.
How do I request a prescription refill?
The fastest way is through the Patient Portal. We process refill requests promptly and notify you when ready. You can also call the office.
Can I see my medical records online?
Yes — the Patient Portal gives you secure access to test results, visit summaries, and other records.
Do you offer telehealth visits?
Yes. We offer virtual visits during business hours for patients who prefer or require remote care.
